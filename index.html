THE STORY

The story this quiz is built on

The Odyssey

Three thousand years ago, Homer told the story of a man named Odysseus. After ten years of war he tried to sail home, and instead spent another ten years lost.

He had no map. He landed on islands that weren't on any chart, met a witch who turned his crew into pigs, a goddess who offered him forever if he would stop trying to leave, and a sea full of voices designed to pull him under. He kept going anyway.

The framework called Life Design takes its name from his journey. Not because anyone here is fighting sea monsters, but because graduate school works the same way: you are moving toward something nobody has fully described to you, and you have to decide before the picture is complete.

Four characters from his story show up in the six scenes ahead

  • A wife who weaves and waits.
  • A blind prophet who sees what others can't yet.
  • A goddess on a hidden island.
  • A hero who builds the cage before the temptation arrives.

One of them is already how you think.
